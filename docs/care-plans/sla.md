# Service Level Agreement (template)

> Template, not legal advice. Only promise what the team can deliver every week.

## Uptime

- **Target:** 99.9% monthly uptime for each hosted Site, measured by our external
  monitor checking the home page every minute.
- **Excluded:** scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours ahead (max 4 hours a
  month, outside 8am to 8pm US Eastern), outages caused by the Client, third-party
  services (domain registrar, DNS the Client manages, payment gateways), and
  attacks beyond reasonable mitigation.

## Service credits

| Monthly uptime | Credit on that month's fee |
| --- | --- |
| 99.0% to 99.9% | 10% |
| 95.0% to 99.0% | 25% |
| Below 95.0% | 50% |

Request credits within 30 days of the month's end. Credits apply to the next
invoice and are the Client's only remedy for missed uptime.

## Support hours and response times

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm US Eastern, excluding US federal
holidays. Requests go to [support@ivulatechnologies.com] or the client portal.

| Priority | Example | Essential | Growth | Commerce |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | Site down, hacked, checkout broken | 2 hours, 24/7 | 1 hour, 24/7 | 30 minutes, 24/7 |
| High | Feature broken, form not sending | 1 business day | Next business day | 4 business hours |
| Normal | Content edit, question | 2 business days | Next business day | 4 business hours |

"Response" means a person has acknowledged the issue and started work, not that
it is fixed. Critical issues are worked continuously until resolved.

## Backups and restores

| Plan | Frequency | Retention | Restore time target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | Daily | 30 days | 1 business day |
| Growth | Daily | 30 days | 4 business hours |
| Commerce | Hourly 8am to 8pm Eastern, plus daily | 30 days | 2 hours |

Backups are stored off-site with a different provider from the hosting server.
We test a restore of every Site at least once a month.
