# Acceptable Use Policy (template)

> Template, not legal advice.

Sites hosted by Ivula must not be used to:

1. Break any law, including US federal and state law and the law where the
   Client operates.
2. Host or link to malware, phishing pages, or tools for unauthorised access.
3. Send unsolicited bulk email or host pages advertised by spam.
4. Infringe copyright, trademarks or other intellectual property. We respond to
   valid DMCA notices sent to our designated agent at [dmca@ivulatechnologies.com].
5. Host child sexual abuse material, content promoting violence or terrorism, or
   content that harasses or threatens people.
6. Sell illegal goods or services, or run gambling or adult content without
   telling us first and holding any required licences.
7. Run crypto-mining, file-sharing hubs, open proxies, or anything that uses
   server resources far beyond a normal business website.
8. Store payment card numbers directly on the Site. Use a PCI-compliant payment
   provider such as Stripe or PayPal.

## What happens if this policy is broken

- **Immediate harm** (malware, phishing, illegal content, an active attack): we
  suspend the Site at once, tell the Client the same day and work with them to
  clean it up.
- **Everything else:** we email the Client, explain the problem and give 7 days
  to fix it before suspending.
- We never delete a Site or its backups without written notice and a chance to
  download them, except where the law requires removal.

Report abuse to [abuse@ivulatechnologies.com].
