This agreement is between **[IVULA US ENTITY NAME, e.g. Ivula Technologies LLC]**, a
[Delaware] limited liability company ("Ivula", "we"), and the customer named on the
order or checkout ("Client", "you"). It starts on the date you subscribe and covers
each website listed on your order ("Site").

## 1. Services

We provide the care plan you choose (Essential, Growth or Commerce) as described
at https://www.ivulatechnologies.com/care-plans on the day you subscribe. Each plan
includes managed hosting, SSL, backups, software updates, uptime monitoring and
the support and edit hours listed for that plan.

**Edit hours** cover content changes, small design tweaks and plugin
configuration. They reset each month and do not roll over. Work beyond the
included hours, redesigns, new features and third-party licence costs are quoted
in writing before we start.

**Not included unless agreed in writing:** content writing, SEO campaigns, paid
plugin or theme licences, email hosting, and fixing problems caused by changes
made by you or another vendor.

## 2. Fees and billing

- Fees are in US dollars, charged in advance each month or year to the card or
  bank account on file through Stripe.
- Annual plans are billed upfront and priced at ten times the monthly fee.
- We may change prices with at least 30 days' written notice; the new price
  applies from your next billing date after that notice.
- If a payment fails, we retry for 14 days. After that we may suspend the Site
  until the balance is paid (see section 6).
- Fees exclude any sales tax that applies in your state; where we must collect
  tax, it is added to your invoice.

## 3. Term and cancellation

- **Monthly plans** renew each month until cancelled. Cancel any time before the
  next billing date; you will not be charged again.
- **Annual plans** renew each year. Cancel any time before the renewal date. If
  you cancel during the first 30 days of an annual plan, we refund the unused
  portion less any migration or edit work already done.
- On cancellation we provide a full backup of the Site files and database within
  5 business days and keep the Site live for 14 days so you can move it.

## 4. Ownership

You own your domain, content, data, designs and any custom code we write for you
under this agreement once paid for. We own our internal tools, server
configuration and processes. We may list your business as a client unless you
ask us not to.

## 5. Service levels

The [Service Level Agreement](./sla.md) sets uptime targets, response times and
service credits. It forms part of this agreement.

## 6. Acceptable use and suspension

You must follow the [Acceptable Use Policy](./acceptable-use-policy.md). We may
suspend a Site without notice if it is compromised, distributing malware,
breaking the law or harming our servers or other clients. For any other
suspension, including unpaid fees, we give at least 7 days' written notice first.
We never delete a Site or its backups because of suspension while the account is
open.

## 7. Your responsibilities

- Keep your account contact details current and respond to our questions.
- Tell us before installing plugins or making code changes yourself.
- Keep your domain registered and renewed (unless we manage it for you).
- Make sure you have the rights to all content you publish on the Site.

## 8. Data protection

We process personal data on the Site only to provide the services and follow
your instructions. We use sub-processors for hosting, backups and payments
([LIST, e.g. DigitalOcean, Cloudways, Stripe]) and will notify you before adding
a new one. We notify you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after
becoming aware of a security breach affecting your data.

## 9. Warranties and liability

We perform the services with reasonable skill and care. Apart from that, the
services are provided "as is". **Our total liability in any 12-month period is
limited to the fees you paid us in that period.** Neither party is liable for
lost profits, lost revenue or indirect damages. Nothing limits liability that
cannot be limited by law.
